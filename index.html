Hi! I am a PhD student in Agricultural and Applied Economics at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign. My research sits at the intersection of development, labor, and household economics, with a particular focus on agricultural contexts and gender.

I am currently studying the effects of the growing agricultural exporting sector in Peru on local economies, gender dynamics, and human capital accumulation.

Before starting my PhD, I worked in various research and public policy institutions, including the Inter-American Development Bank and the University of Oxford, where I also completed the MSc in Economics for Development.
